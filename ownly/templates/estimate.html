How the estimate works

{{ valuer.model_name }} learned what Ames homes sold for in 2006–2010 from 2,925 sales. It prices this home as if it sold in mid-2010. The FHFA house price index for Ames then moves that price to today: published through {{ valuer.today().latest_label }}, and extended at the two-year trend of {{ '%.1f' | format(valuer.today().growth * 100) }}% a year after that.

On houses it had never seen, the model was within about 7% on average, and 8 in 10 sold inside the likely range. The step from 2010 to today adds uncertainty that can't be measured without recent sales, so treat this as a guide, not an appraisal.
